### Water

- **D494** (with D280, D387 (2), D394, D457, D491): **Remove unfed water on maps with caves works on water columns, not tiles.** Water is fed when a running source, or a stored lake the removal doesn't take, reaches it by the game's flow rule along the stacked engine's column graph, direction limiters and partial obstacles included. A removal takes the unfed water of every column on the tiles it names and never fed water, so a fed river over an unfed cave pool keeps the river and loses the pool. An unfed pool (columns joined by edges, all wet and unfed) with any column on a named tile goes whole, and so does any other unfed pool with a column on a tile that pool covers; an unfed Fill sharing a tile goes with it. The water then settles on from there, and the shortcut that reuses the last settle is never used once a map has a removal. Built in #498 (2026-10-25), recorded by the milestone session.
