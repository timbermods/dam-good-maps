### The look

- **D497** (amends D280 (2) and D135): **Terrain is lit as the game lights it.** Draw it as the game draws it; editing aids get their own channel (layers, marks, outlines), never a changed look. The game renders in Gamma colour space (`globalgamemanagers` PlayerSettings m_ActiveColorSpace 0), so its terrain shader (TerrainURP, read from the game's `resources.assets`) works on display values: a face's colour is its albedo × 0.912 (URP's diffuse share at `_Metallic` 0.05) × (the ambient by its normal + 1.1 × the sun's colour × max(n·L, 0) × its shadow), with a little specular. The ambient is Unity's Trilight (decompiled `Timberborn.SkySystem` `Sun`: `RenderSettings.ambientMode = AmbientMode.Trilight`; the day's colours in `Configurations/Sun.blueprint.json`: sky 0.62/0.63/0.97 from above, equator white from the side, ground 0.28/0.23/0.37 from below) as Unity's ambient probe, unoccluded: irradiance SH (amended 2026-10-29 (planning): follows the game, D496's rule, the finish): the gradient, blended in linear values by a direction's height, projected to L2 spherical harmonics with the clamped cosine's convolution baked in, as the engine does for every ambient source, so a top takes about 0.78/0.79/0.98, a wall 0.84/0.83/0.91 and an underside 0.65/0.64/0.68 (`palette.ts` `GAME_AMBIENT_SH`); the engine's projection is not in the game's files, so a frame capture of the SH the game uploads (unity_SHAr..SHC) is still to check it, fix forward; only the sun's shadow (strength 1) darkens a face; `GraphicsQualitySystem` has no ambient-occlusion setting. Its height terms: the ground's altitude colour ramps (`TerrainMaterialMap`: twice the ramp's texel at the ground's height over `AltitudeCeiling` 22; `AltitudeMultiplierGrass_Manual` on grass, `AltitudeMultiplierDesert_Manual` on dry ground, before contamination), and the cliffs' gradient (Cliff, CliffEdge, SlopeCliff: their stone × a tint from (0.416, 0.431, 0.475) at level 0 to (0.941, 0.925, 0.894) at level 22). The probe's in-game shots of roofed terrain show floors under arches and roofs lit flat, as any shade. So both looks light the terrain in display values by that ambient by the normal and the sun by its shadow, with no sky-visibility darkening, no contact shadow and no ambient occlusion; tops take the altitude ramp; a cave's interior, an overhang's and an arch's take the same light as open ground, the sun only where it reaches. D280 (2)'s "a little murky" goes, and D135's ambient occlusion in High. Caves still read while editing through the hollow under the cursor (#528), the Caves layer and the cut, which need no darkness; where something reads less well, the answer is an editing aid in its own channel. Its colours (amended 2026-10-29 (planning): follows the game, D496's rule; #722): the colours the light multiplies are the game's: our ground's and walls' patterns keep their shapes, and their mean albedo, measured in the page with the light off (`tools/albedo-means.ts`), is the game's texture mean (`resources.assets`, the 64² mip, read raw): dry ground the `Desert` texture's (TerrainMaterialMap.blueprint.json `DesertTexture`) and grass the `Grass` texture's, each times its detail texture doubled (`GrassDetailsAlbedo`, mean 140: × 1.098), so 69/57/46 and 74/80/27 of 255; walls the `CliffDetail` texture's, 68/62/52, times the cliffs' gradient, in both looks. The walls' level bands (D135's "height as layered bands on walls", D324's and D334's every other level a shade darker) go with it: the game's cliffs have none, and they were a readability aid baked into the look, which this rule sends to its own channel: Markers' level lines count levels. Its finish (amended 2026-10-29 (planning): follows the game, D496's rule; the game's scene, `level2`'s RenderSettings, and its `Rendering/Volume` profile), in every look, in place of our own (Standard's warm tint, High's tone curve, grade and haze, and their switches): done, the exponential fog (`m_FogMode` 2, density 0.003, as URP's FOG_EXP programs draw it: `exp2(-density / √ln 2 × the view depth)`, toward the day's fog colour or a held hazard's; none looking straight down); done, `m_ReflectionIntensity` 0.65 on the environment's reflection (the game's probe, `sharedassets1.assets` "TimberbornEnvironmentRelfectionProbe", at its roughest mip on the terrain, the water's at its own roughness) with the sun's GGX highlight (the light look, drawn in software, leaves out the terrain's: a few hundredths of the light); done, the post chain (URP's LDR grading under Gamma: +0.15 EV, Neutral tonemapping, contrast +14 round ACEScc's mid grey, saturation +3); unsettled, `m_AmbientIntensity` 1.2, not applied (Unity's Lighting window offers the Intensity Multiplier for Skybox ambient only, and the game sets Trilight; but the shade measurements point to it applying: against the formula the game's shots are about 1.08× in sun and 1.3× in shade, an ambient about 1.2× short; the frame capture of the SH decides). Still to follow: the frame capture of the ambient SH, and bloom (threshold 1.5: highlights only).
