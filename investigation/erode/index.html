Click rock to wear it, or drag along a cliff or across a ridge. Right-drag turns, middle-drag pans, the wheel zooms. Esc takes a playing erode back; Ctrl+Z undoes one.

Water under the new roofs is an approximation until the water engine lands: a hollow fills to the level of the water beside it, with no pressure or flow. The rest of the water is the map's own.
