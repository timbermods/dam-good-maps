## Intention

- A waterfall plunges off a cliff into a large, roughly round lake (Kyler's own). **Emerged** (a lake of 491 tiles of open water (axes 0.58, fill 0.46) with a fall of 12 levels into it).

## How it plays

- You start by a lake, 3 tiles away on your level.
- It lasts through the first drought; in a 26-day Hard drought it is gone by day 18.
- A 14-tile dam 9 tiles east holds a drought's water.
- Badwater lies 19 tiles west.
- 156 logs of wood within 20 tiles' walk, mostly pine and birch: little wood a tree, quick to regrow; plus about 50 logs growing.
- A waterfall plunges off a cliff into a big, round lake.
- Expand to the east and south, on some level land.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics, another lake or river, waterfalls and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** mesa, plateau, cone, trough, basin, escarpment over land falling toward the south.
- **Relief:** 15 levels (p5–p95), 7 levels in use, highest 16; 28% cliff, 57% flat; tallest fall 12.01 levels.
- **Water:** 3 rivers (1 from the edge, 2 from springs), 2 lakes, 3 falls, a river island; water covers 11% of the map.
- **Reach:** 16% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 84% needs stairs (8 natural ramps, 1 upland left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** a hollow on high ground, draining by its own ditch.

## Name

**Badwater Cone**, by the names study's rule `badwater-cone`: A cone frames a contained badwater basin. Keep colonies on the clean side while you manage its leveed outlet.
