## How it plays

- You start by a river, 3 tiles away on your level.
- It lasts through the first drought; in a 26-day Hard drought it is gone by day 8.
- A 6-tile dam 24 tiles west holds a drought's water.
- Thorns bar the way north-east, 30 tiles out.
- 118 logs of wood within 20 tiles' walk, mostly oak: plenty of wood, slow to regrow; plus about 50 logs growing.
- Expand to the north and west, on narrow ledges.
- Most of the high ground needs stairs.
- Further out: mine sites, a geothermal field, relics and most of the ruins.

## Terrain

- **Land:** basin, mesaField over a bowl draining toward the north.
- **Relief:** 12 levels (p5–p95), 16 levels in use, highest 15; 11% cliff, 32% flat; tallest fall 6.11 levels.
- **Water:** 4 rivers (2 from the edge, 2 from springs), 3 lakes, 3 falls; water covers 15% of the map.
- **Reach:** 1% of the dry land is reached on foot from the start; 99% needs stairs (0 natural ramps, 2 uplands left to stairs).
- **Badwater:** none.

## Name

**Boughs Cascade**, by the names study's rule `fallback:boughs+cascade`: The start has clean water within reach. Follow the main river and choose a nearby place to store water before drought.
