# D357 readiness: custom map sizes

Audited `dev` at `7adabb48d2017a428a5a08ecf49d19e8f427cfc2` in the isolated `dgm-custom-sizes` clone on 2026-10-05. Product files are unchanged. This is an investigation, not an implementation or game-load approval. References are ROADMAP.md lines 813–832 and docs/decisions/future.md line 3. File/line references below are for that commit. `src/` and `rust/` were inventoried and searched for side/area limits, coordinate and index encodings, square reconstruction, fixed allocation, presets and shape/size scaling; matches were traced into their readers and callers. Constants that are heights, colors, hashes, force settings or byte formats are distinguished from map dimensions below.

## Blockers, ranked by amount of work (largest first)

1. **Large: shape-aware land, starts and must-haves, especially the minimum sizes.** The code accepts rectangles in its data structures, but it has no long-axis policy for rivers, canyons, island chains or the start's journey. Many theme lengths use the shorter side and many placement bands assume room around a square-sized start. Area alone chooses mines and district intentions. A 4-tile-wide map cannot contain a 5×5 mine site; a 4×4 map cannot meet the existing survival floor (even 16 fully grown oaks give only 128 logs, below 178, before reserving the start, water or food). Lifting the schema cannot resolve D357's simultaneous “every width/height from 4” and “absolutes never relax.” This requires an explicit product resolution at adoption, not a silent relaxation in this report.
2. **Medium: widen saved feature/edit contracts together with their runtime checks.** Coordinates, brush dabs and tile indices have independent 256-era schema bounds. A force can play successfully yet its operation can be refused by the document; do not mistake the raw core readings for working editing/history. Selection and area-operation caps and the brush radius also need attention. Preserve old projects and links while expanding the same numeric encodings; no new compact binary format is needed.
3. **Small: public gates, presets/warning and a latent square-width default.** MapSpec, share decoder, validator and size controls have explicit limits. Named shapes need two dimensions instead of a scalar preset. The writer already handles the requested shapes. The optional four-line adoption patch makes `fieldData` require its true width; it does not lift any limits or implement D357.
