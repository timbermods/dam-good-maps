> **Held (Kyler, 2026-10-01, D370):** Highlands is already strong, and its 96² gain costs nearly three times the first-land time; re-audited after M9b's shared fixes, then decided. Its shared findings (D348 broken on every map, river joins that settle apart) go to the M9b agent.


## Shared findings for the M9b agent

- **D348: terrain changes after first land on all 60 baseline maps.** One land is shown;
  45–103 / 34–93 / 64–324 tiles subsequently change at 96² / 128² / 256².
  `gen/generate.ts:1613` calls `onLand` before `badAt` (1621, 1927, 1981) and
  `levelStart` (1995). These shared paths mutate the shown height field; they are not
  land replacement, but still breach terrain identity outside the permitted outlet wear.
  The unchanged measures record this separately from blocking validators. The prototype
  inherits it (96²: 47–151 changed tiles); this patch supplies no shared fix.
- **Planned joins can become disconnected settled water.** Baseline 128² seed 3 passes
  planned water, but two tributary heads occupy separate wet systems; their declared
  exits join the main river. Captured courses contain one/two-sample dry gaps.
  At 256² seed 12, a `plug-lake` map's dominant lake leaves five heads outside its system
  (main share 25%); one joining course has a 12-sample dry stretch. Trace evidence is
  regenerated by `causes.py`. Suspect the shared hydrology/settle and plug/story interaction;
  the traces locate the failure, not a proven simulator fix.
- **Shared selection and time remain limiting.** The six/three-plan screen cap can admit
  promise misses; 96² seeds 5/15 also fail the planned promise yet pass it settled.
  Baseline 128² settled-map median/p90 is 5.21/8.28s against D333's 2/5s;
  256² first land is 8.83/17.09s against 3/6s, water 15.46/36.89s against 8/20s.
  Seed 19 at 256² needs 13 settles and about 102s overall. Shared repair/selection work
  needs M9b's attention; no thresholds or shared code change here.

## Prototype disposition

[adoption.patch](adoption.patch) strengthens only small Highlands' flow, incision and bank
floor prior; it tapers to zero change at 128. The other themes, Any, and larger Highlands
retain their draws. At 96², six misses recover and seed 15 regresses (82% wet main course,
including a 16-sample dry stretch). A broader trial regressed 128² and was discarded.

**Hold adoption for timing and shared absolutes.** Concurrent machine load makes raw timing
comparisons uneven, but measures' CPU-adjusted 96² first-land median also rises, 2.46 → 3.41s.
The improvement is useful evidence, not a quality/performance pass.
