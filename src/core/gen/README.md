# gen

The generator: settings in, a map out. `generate` draws a genome, grows the land and its rivers (`land/`), reads the features back out of them, picks the start, plants resources and objects, then builds, validates and writes the file.

**Rules**
- Deterministic (PLAN §2.1): the same seed and settings give the same file everywhere. Random numbers come from named streams (`math/`).
- A change that alters a default map needs one deliberate re-pin of the tests and golden fixtures (D308) and a new `GENERATOR_VERSION` (`spec/mapspec.ts`).
- Themes lean the generator; they are not templates (D208).
- A candidate that fails a check is retried, up to `MAX_ATTEMPTS`; the first that passes is the map, never swapped (D329). `versions.ts` looks for a sibling that meets all three outcomes, in the background; `showcase.ts` (Showcase mode, D488 (2), asked for explicitly) searches siblings the same way for the time allowed, scores each by its arc (six beats measured on the finished map, `arcOf`, `arcScore`) and the looks of its opening picture (`looksOf`, `analysis/picture.ts`, D506) and keeps the best three that pass every absolute, each with its share spec and a note in a player's words (`showcaseSearch`; `tried` reproduces it).
- Badwater distance (`hazards.badwaterDistance`) is the start's badwater rule too (D527): one value the hollows (`land/hazards.ts`, with Easy start's where farther), the settler's start and the checks read.
- Clean water only (planning's condition of 2026-11-17, #236): a start whose nearest water, walked from it over the map's own slopes as `start.water` walks to its clean water, is too foul to pump (contamination `PUMP_CLEAN` or more) gives way to another on the same settled water (`generate.ts` `startBadWaterWalk`), and a land where none passes is refused with that reason ("start water bad"). What a start is worth is measured in `startValue.ts` (bench, walkable land, moist ground for its wood, ways out, water against the rule's aim), for the land stage's seat (#1227); the settler's picker puts room first, never at the cost of an Easy row (`settler.ts`, D545): on the settled water it finds the place its ranking before would choose (its score alone, the seed's same draw), then takes the places whose own level room (`roomAt`, Easy start's room row: the dry tiles at the start's level joined to it within 14 tiles, the start well inside them) meets Easy's ask and that keep the Easy rows that place keeps (the water walk from the middle, at most `WALK_SPARE` tiles longer; the badwater sources' hollows at Easy's distance; the moist ground for the wood), else those keeping them with at least that place's room, else that place; among them each place's score, the seed's draw in it too, is scaled by what its room and the moist ground for its wood are worth (`startWorth`), and the seed draws only among the best few whose worth stands within `VALUE_MARGIN` (0.15) of the worthiest. On the water the hydrology planned (the guess the badwater hollows are planned away from) it ranks as before (`roomFirst: false`). On the settled water it reads a place's walks over the slopes the build will place (`SettlerOptions.slopeWater`: toward the rivers' channel, off the start's `START_CLEAR_RADIUS` clearing; the river's steps cached by `slopes.ts` `stepsFrom`), so its walk is the one the start's water row measures, and the room lists aim the walk within Easy's water row, half a tile to spare (`walkWantRoom`); the first place and the plan's guess keep the rule's aim (D545 round 3, generator 0.10.52). Where the place a room list draws shows less of the map than `FRAME_FULL` in its opening frame (D507's `landInFrame`, a start against the map's edge), the list's best few that keep every Easy row it keeps are drawn from again with their scores and worth weighed by their frames, and that draw's place is taken where its frame shows `FRAME_GAIN` more (D545 round 4).
- Every setting the panel allows makes a map (D471): a round whose attempts are spent is followed by rescue rounds (`rescueRounds`) on fresh lands (a shown land whose start finds no place twice in a row ends its round early and is kept for the first rescue round, so the land shown stays the map; above 128², where a land is shown before its start is proved, one that fails the same way three times in a row, or has cost 10 settles in the round, ends its round as spent attempts do, `SHOWN_SAME`, `LAND_SETTLES`, and is kept for the first rescue round too (generator 0.10.23); only a round after that draws fresh land, D471's one exception to D348), without the land screens or drawn intentions, each easing more (`GenerateOptions.ease` lists them: soft targets such as the start's joined land, and the badwater distance: no more than the map's size allows and a seventh less each round, never under 15 tiles or the player's own when nearer (`easedBadwater`; the hollows, the start and the generator's own check hold the eased distance, the map's spec keeps the player's), a start levelled from further or nearer the edge, the exact Rivers count, then from the fourth round the land drawn nearer the theme's own terrain and water and from the eighth gentle land, under 128² at the map's own scale, `easedSettings`); the absolutes and the spec never change. Up to 48 rounds on the smallest maps. The start's water aims within the rule less 2, never under the 3 tiles a dry 5×5 allows (`settler.ts` `waterAim`); under a rule of 7, where a tile decides, the settler reads a place's water from its middle, as the check walks, a place made ready as the land was shaped is checked on the settled water before a settle is spent on it, and a bank start's levelled path ends at water a pump draws, within the rule's walk (generator 0.10.29); in a rescue round, a start-planting mix that can't give Minimum starting wood plants the start's groves with oak, the species that gives the most wood, a start whose 20 tiles' walk holds less dry land than that wood and those berries need gives way to another, and what the start rules ask that the planting left short is filled in densely on the colony's walk. In a rescue round, a shown land whose settled water left no start of the land it should join asks only for Buildable land's. Settings away from the theme's own keep their promise on the start (generator 0.10.22, `tests/contract/settings.test.ts`): a Drought reserve above the theme's own draws a planned land again, within the land screens, while its planned water holds less stored water than the reserve asks (`reserveShort`: the best dam site's reservoir or the water a drought keeps, as `water.storage_possible` reads them), and any moved reserve has the settler read a place's stored water that way (`reserveAsked`), a reserve below the theme's own passing over places that hold more than it asks; Buildable land Generous, where the theme's own is less, has the start walk to its 2,500 tiles (by area under 128², as the floors scale) outside rescue rounds, and Buildable land moved off the theme's own chooses among the starts that pass every floor (`REACH_CHOICES` looked at) the one walking to the least land for Tight and the most for Generous; at the theme's own the first that passes stands. `tests/contract/everySetting.ts` holds the panel's extremes, run nightly.
- A join never costs a land (D534): a plan refused on a check that names or touches a joined course is planned again on the same land without the joins; any other refusal draws the next land as dev does. The joined courses (`land/hydro.ts` `Hydro.joinedRivers`) are each course joined to one beside it, the course it joined, the course a refused head would have run beside and every course traced after that head; a refusal names one (`joinCaused`) as a leak's course, a drowned head, the main river a promise or water story is read on, a source in a flow's course or one whose water reaches it, a course whose water came down to a badwater hollow, or a built check's feature or tiles within `JOIN_TOUCH` of one. The second plan (`Land.joinsOff`, `HydroOptions.joins`) comes only on a land not yet shown (a land shown stays the map), its screening count and dry tally put back as they were before the first, its genome as the refused plan left it (a Canyon whose joined plan missed its promise is planned again a level deeper on a lower floor, the promise screen's step).
- Small maps at their own scale (D541, generator 0.10.40): a map narrower than 96 (`OWN_SCALE_SIDE`) and under 128² of area draws its land at its own scale from the first round (`land/genome.ts` `ownScaleFor`, `DrawOptions.ownScale` "root"): its parts by area, its levels' spread, its rivers' incision and hanging valleys by the root of its narrower side over 128, its extra lakes by the root of its area; Highlands keeps a 128²'s land until the eighth round, when every theme's gentle land under 128² takes the ratio itself ("side"). 96² and up are a 128²'s land as before.
- The first land shown is the map (D348): a land passes the land stage (`planLandStage`: its rivers planned, its courses checked) and every check the land alone can judge (no inflow's head under a lake, `land/courses.ts` `drownedHeads`; no wall along an edge; a start on the planned water as its land holds it, `plannedWater` with `held` (lakes no higher than their rim, rivers spread over their floodplain), and a second place for one away from it (where that water leaves no place or one, at 128² and under, on it and the pre-fill's water together: the plan's streams run too shallow for a pump and miss the pools the settled water backs into, so deep-cut Canyon and Highlands lands with places were drawn again); room for the mine sites, D363; no sea over a shelf of more than 10,000 tiles at its spill level or mostly on one over a quarter of the map, no land whose own water floods past its theme's line (`water.no_flood`, D348; planning's ruling on #236, 2026-11-15): where the planned water reaches half the line (`FLOOD_LOOK`), the pre-fill of the rivers and lakes alone, before the start, the badwater and the settle, is read, and a land over the line has its water planned again, then is drawn again (D530, "a flooded land (planned)"; Any 256² seed 4's lakes stood over 61% of the map against 55% on six attempts on dev 0.10.29; `info.ownFlood` the reading). A river that backs up along a flat bed over its banks, a shallow sheet over its floodplain, is not refused (its channel cut, D530, is deferred for speed); no ground above 16 unless tall, no ruler-straight channel or dam wall on its planned water, its lakes' banks read alone too, no dam wall on the pre-fill's water 0.2 deep or more), then the prepared land is kept privately while its water, start and mine pair are proved (#153: an exhausted hidden land may be drawn again); `onLand` fires, once per map (editable land), once the actual settled start reaches the required pair (at 128² and under, `SHOW_PROVED_MOST`; above, at once, as D278's time to editable land asks, but for a long Islands map, whose land waits for its sea to settle), and it is never replaced. Room and placement share one keep-off mask (`extras.ts` `objectKeepOff`) and the nearest-footprint distance (`resources/mineGround.ts`). After its water settles: no start on the settled water takes the plan's start or level dry ground (`dryStart`), a start without water gets a spring by it (`springByStart`, D330's fix; `info.fixes` says which), a start that still fails gives way to another on the same settled water (`START_TRIES`), and what fails is planned again on the same land, keeping off the starts that failed. The badwater hollows are dug before the land is shown and kept on every attempt, and the dry washes cut after them (D517, `land/washes.ts`: off every place a start may take and its ground, the hollows, the mine sites and the natural ramps; across the bench plates by the water a start the settled water gives could take only while each keeps its bench, `benchPlates`; their fords join the land's ramps); a start on the shown land needs no levelling but as a last resort; so the land shown is the land at the end, byte for byte, but for the worn way out (`tests/contract/firstLand.test.ts`). Water that doesn't settle has a plug holding its lake opened, then its way out worn wider (`water/outletWear.ts`, D350 (b)); only when that fails too do the attempts stop (`stuck`).
- Long maps (D357, D488): a long Lake Basin whose land is still hidden (128² and under) and whose settled water misses the promise (a dry basin, a lake drained to a pool) has its land drawn again, up to `DRY_BASIN_REDRAWS` times; a long Canyon's planned gorge is the main river's down the length (`gorged`), whatever the width. A long Islands map's land is kept hidden until its sea has settled, and a sea that drained as it settled (under `DRAINED_SEA` of the map or `SETTLED_SEA` of the water planned) draws another land, sharing those redraws; a long map's open sea is drawn inside it (`land/genome.ts` `inBasin`), and Any keeps its seas and bowls there.
- Thin sheets (D333, the pooled probe): a lake's shelf at its spill level is cut a level lower at the land stage (`lowerShelves`, not round a sea), and a tributary runs a level under a floor a bigger river cleared (`land/hydro.ts`), so no flat stands level with the water beside it.
- Mine sites' ground (D363): as the land is shaped, before it is shown, `land/minePads.ts` `minePads` makes sure the planned start's walk holds level squares for its mine sites 24 tiles or more out, levelling a pad (a level down at most, about 49 tiles, its edge eased into the land) where it has too few; the hollows keep off those squares and the ways to them, and on the settled water the start goes where its walk has that room (`roomMap`).
- Sources: None (D330): `withoutSources` makes the map as usual, then removes its sources and their water (the field's `dry`).
- The opening shot (D493): the start is turned so the game's first camera (60° down, turned to the orientation + 35°, judged pulled back to the default zoom's widest) frames the best view: among the orientations whose door is level, a landmark in view first (`analysis/opening.ts` `bestTurn`, `openingShotScore`), then falls, open water and relief, with the share of the frame that shows the map rather than the void past its edge (`landInFrame`, D507), the door nearest the water breaking ties. Among the good few places that keep the start's rules, one whose frame shows clearly more of the map, with as good an opening shot, is taken (`gen/settler.ts` `FRAME_GAIN`, D507). On a land that stands up (Highlands above 128², D547 step 5) only the turns showing the least badwater are weighed, where every turn of the place drawn shows some the place among the good few whose turn shows the least is taken, and the swap never takes a frame showing more (`badView`, `analysis/opening.ts` `tilesInFrame`). The landmark intention is a standing form 8+ levels over its ring, 25–60 tiles from the start, in that frame's upper half (or a fall of 6+ within 60); the settler prefers places that frame one (`land/intentions.ts` `startPreference`) and re-steers once. Every other map leans toward one too, at half that pull, and on River Valley, Lake Basin and Any grows the intention's form, one per whole 128² of the map (D502, `land/genome.ts` `BACKDROP_THEMES`). Showcase mode reads the same functions. `tools/opening-rates.ts` measures how often frames hold a landmark.
- Islands' start above 128² (D547's Islands round 8, generator 0.10.55; an island sea whose narrower side is over 128, its land 0.10.48's, `UPLANDS_BASE_PATH`): the map's slopes follow the ringed land's rules (`ringSlopes`, `features/slopes.ts` `RING_SLOPES`), so the start walks its island without stairs as far as its one-level steps reach, while the pick reads its places' walks as before (`pickRings`), so the place, its room and its rows are the pick's own; the plan's place made ready as the land was shaped is never the start where its ground is under `minFoot` (`isleStart`, and `planFooted`, which reads the settled water again with that ground as the floor first); the start is turned to the view with the least badwater, never moved for it (`settler.ts` `badTurnOnly`); and on Normal its berries are topped up to Easy's food row, 44 within 15 tiles' walk on ground that stays moist through a Normal drought (`resources.ts` `startFood`, as Easy's top-up plants them), so the slopes it adds, which move the patches the planting draws, never cost the start its food.
- Lake Basin above 128² (its hydro pass, generator 0.10.57; `g.basinWarp`, set only by `land/lakeBasin.ts` `sidedBasin`): the lake owns the basin's lowest levels (`land/hydro.ts`, `land/README.md`), and the land stage brings one stretch of the big lake's shore to a level over its water (`shoreBench`, after the rivers and before `lakesAtSpill` plans the lakes again on the finished land): up to `BENCH_WIDE` tiles back from the water where the land stands at most a level over it and two under, its width drawn from noise along the shore, widest near its middle and pinched at its ends, off the channels and the map's edge, the land a level higher behind it the step its slopes cross. The basin's land steps a level at a time round the lake, and without it no lakeside place held a start's room (256² seeds 6 and 8: 316 and 330 tiles; with it 562 and 538). The start then gives way where its walk, on its level and over the slopes as `start.reach` reads it, reaches less than the pick's own floor (`minFoot`), its walk region marked tried so the next is not on the same shelf; `BASIN_START_TRIES` (6) starts are tried on one settled water; and a start whose opening view shows badwater gives way on every try, the last turned to a view without it where one has none, or the land is planned again ("badwater in the start's view"). Its badwater keeps `BASIN_BAD_FAR` (90) tiles off the plan's start, and its slopes follow the ringed land's rules (`ringSlopes`, round 7c).
- Easy start (D495, `settings.start.easyStart`): its targets (`analysis/easyStart.ts` `EASY_START`) tighten the start rules the map is made and checked by (the result's spec keeps the player's); the settler plans the start where the land gives room and water 13–24 tiles off (`settler.ts` `easy`); as the land is shaped the start and every place it may fall back on get their clearing and backwater pond on the planned water (`land/easyStart.ts` `shapeEasyStart`, cut on a copy and kept only whole, off the ground the others took; the pond, its cut and mouth kept as water is), a place that can't have them dropped; no start is accepted or thrown away on the plan's estimate (D453, generator 0.10.42, `generate.ts` `easyStartOn`): on the land's settle the plan's shapes are tried first as they stand, and one that passes every start check is kept, the map settled once; where none holds, and on the last of a land's tries, they are taken out of the land again (`planShapes`; put back where no place is then found) and a place is shaped on the settled water (`easySeatAt`, `shapeEasyStart` with `settled`: at a plan's place or within 6 tiles of it, the widest clearing that keeps the start's bench, places with still water round them first, then any place the settler finds on the settled water) and its land settled once more, so a place the water moved from costs a re-pick and never a new land; the start's land and water targets are read on its build before anything is planted (`EASY_LAND`, the build given the start's entity, `startEntity`), a start that misses one giving the land back as it was and giving way to the next place on the same settled water, its berries go on drought-proof ground and a ruin field on its walk, on dry ground or, where no field fits there, on the green (`resources.ts` `easy`), thorns keep 30 tiles off (on every map Designed for Easy too, Easy start on or off, though the checks run these rows only with Easy start on: `extras.ts` `EASY_THORNS`, uplands round 6b step 5); the land is shown only once the map passed, and a land that missed is planned again; a map a late rescue round made carries what it left short of the full targets (`GenerateResult.easyShort`, the checks' `easyShortfall`) into its project for the page to say. Off, every path is the one before it.
- 3D forms (D488, D503): on a map that passed, `forms3d.ts` `formsFor` draws which forms it looks for (a spring cave, an overhang by the start, an arch over a gorge, an arch worn through rock, an overhanging cliff), `land/forms3d.ts` grows them where the land offers a place, and each is kept only where every check still holds (and the dead stands' bare land, D516: a spring cave whose water moistens dry land past its share is dropped; a spring cave whose stream would cross a planned lake is dropped before it is built), it stands as grown and it reads as what it is, as the game's camera sees it (`analysis/seeThrough.ts`: an arch open through both ways, an arch over a gorge both ways along its gorge, along x, y or the diagonal a winding gorge runs at the crossing, D543; an overhang's roof two or more tiles deep); one that cannot is dropped and recorded, never forced (D138). The living account is `docs/progress/3d-generation.md`.
- Green over the whole map (D504): above 128² the springs toward the land far from water read it by the soil's own reach (`land/hydro.ts` `moistCost`) and have heads of their own, two for each 128² beyond the first, and a readable story's counts (heads, other systems, rivers of their own) are a 128²'s for each 128² of the map (`analysis/story.ts` `storyScale`). `tools/bare-share.ts` measures the bare land (dry, no moisture, no plant: the game's desert texture over the whole top) per map, its barest 64² block and its largest bare square; `tools/contact-sheet.ts --soil` draws the ground as the game shows its soil.
- Real places are never generator input (D108): nothing here reads `places/`.

**Start from**
- `generate.ts` `generate`. A spec whose `premise` names one (share link key `p`) goes to the premise mode first
  (`premise/generate.ts` `generatePremise`, docs/PREMISES.md); when it says why it cannot, the themes make the map
  (`generateThemes`) and `info.premise.fellBack` holds the reason. A start without water gets a spring at a place by `water/springSites.ts` `springCandidates`, the candidate rule `doc/waterFix.ts` shares (each keeps its own walk, filters, tries and strengths).
- `settler.ts` `pickStart` (the start rules); `intentions.ts` (checks on the finished map); `resources.ts` `planResources` (dead stands on the dry land by `deadStands.ts` `planDeadStands`, D516, on the old water line, the washes' beds (`beds`, D517), mesa tops' rims and dry hollows, two to four woods grown outward from their middles, at most a quarter of the dry land within 6 tiles of a dead tree (`DEAD_STANDS.bare`), each stand's knots by `clumpStand` (D518); on `resources/baseline.ts`'s planners and its shared rules: `woodPerTree`, `succulentsOf`, `saplings`, `NEAR_WALK`, `startWalkField`; Grove size near the start (planning's ruling on #236, 6899389318, generator 0.10.29): away from Normal each grove the start's own planting grows takes the setting's size (`startSize`, every pass alike, never cut to the wood still wanted), Big Woods' growing on across broken ground a beaver walks to from the start (`baseline.ts` `growGroveAcross`, the gaps up to 2 tiles) and Scattered's small (at most 1.5 times the median) with a tile of open ground between them (`startGap`); at Normal, and in a rescue round (`lean`), the start's planting is as it was); `outcomes.ts` `PROMISES` (each theme's promise, its version-note words and its land noun, one table; judged against `promiseSides`: the short side on a map that is not long, and on a long map (D488) the long side for a line along the course, the side of a square of its area for a share of the land, the short side for a width across the river); `extras.ts` `planExtras` (mine sites, relics, thorns); `resources/baseline.ts` `planRuinFields` with `follow` (the generator's ruin fields lie in bands along their level's rim, `rimDistance`, ragged with gaps, and the ruins on a rise on a disc broken at its edge, `ruinRagged`: D518, the look round); `frameStand.ts` `planFrameStand` (a stand of the map's ruins whole in the opening view pulled back, ahead and up, on dry ground, planned last in `planResources` from the start's turn, `turn`, and kept past the two-ways intention's reach, `standBeyond`; its scrap given back by the other fields, `giveBackScrap`: D493, amended 2026-11-20); `ruinStories.ts` `planRuinStories` (ruins placed with meaning, D488 (2): a ruined dam across the natural narrows, a drowned village; each field's line in `params.story.say`, read by `ruinStories`); `weir.ts` `planWeir`.
- `valley.ts` is a remnant: `startWalkable`, kept only for the frozen design prototypes the equivalence tools import.
- `calibrated.ts` (official-map targets, mirrors `prototype/calibrated.py`); `pack.ts` `worldOf` (the one path to file bytes, PLAN §19.7: the generator's `toTimberFile`, Real places' `buildPlace` and the probe's test maps (both on `places/place.ts` `buildFileFromHeights`) and an opened map's export all write their world through it).

**Tests**: `tests/contract/` (calibrated, parity, share, resources, badwater, outflows, start) and `tests/e2e/determinism.spec.ts`. Batches: `tools/batch.ts`, `tools/batches.ts`. Run `npx vitest run tests/contract/calibrated.test.ts`.
