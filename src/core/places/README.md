# places

Real places: maps made from real terrain by the landscape survey, offered as content to open or refine (D136). This folder holds the place data format and the credits every place carries.

**Rules**
- Never generator input (D108): nothing under `gen/` or `features/` reads a place.
- A place keeps its real land (D245, D331). It is built with the same steps and code as a generated map, under the game's water rules (D311's conversion, D521): it opens with the water the game keeps, so the first edit, or the game's first days, takes nothing away (`buildPlace`; `setPlaceRules("port")` builds it as before, for a tool's comparison).
- Water where the real water lies (D271, D521, `tools/lib/placeBasins.ts`): the observed water (ESA WorldCover 2021, `investigation/landscapes/library/real-water.json`, written by `tools/places-real-water.ts`, credited in every place) decides the land's only changes. A body of 300 tiles or more the port's rules showed and the game's lose, or a film above floor 0 (a body of 100 tiles or more, 0.11 deep or less on every tile, mostly not in a bed: `tools/lib/placeWater.ts` `films`), is cut one level down on its real-water tiles inside its flat's own rim, so the place's rivers fill it; where no real water lies a lost body goes, and a film gets a bed one level down from where its water comes in, across the flat it stands on, to where the flat lets it out. A floor-0 sheet the edge's spill holds (D303) stays, as the game keeps it, and is counted. The check's film is thin on 90 % of its tiles (D(new-films)); the films that share finds besides are left to the springs' search, and those it leaves get the same basin or bed after it (`filmsAfter`), each kept only where the water still settles, nothing the place passed then fails and the real water is held (a place whose leftover film has no such mend keeps today's build, `TODAY_KEPT`, and so does one whose search would end with less water over real water than today's, a fault today's has not, or more water over real dry land than today's that its start does not need: D(new-films)); a bed the searched springs leave dry gives its land back.
- The springs are searched under the game's rules (D521, D(new-films), `tools/lib/placeFeeds.ts`) by D271's measure, the water over real water against the water over real dry land: by turns each source keeps, runs weaker, goes or moves to the head of the real water; a place with no real water (no body of 30 tiles in the mask) is also searched with D245's water floor first, one spring for all (one of its own, or a spring by the start), and keeps the better; the one spring is the start's: its water within the start's walk and no farther than before, a pool the start pumps from with the first pump (`spring.ts` `startPool`), a spring above the start (`aboveStart`) tried first, then the nearest pool; a start still without pumpable water once every spot is tried gets the start's spring by the same rule; where no single change gains, two or three sources go together. A change is kept when it gains 20 tiles, the real water is held (a turn may lose 2 % of it, the one spring and the group none), and nothing it passed then fails, the start's water first; every place whose springs change logs its start's numbers before and after (its water in reach first). The measure splits, it does not forgive: the water over real dry land is printed per place and in the run's totals as the floor-0 sheet no deeper than 0.11 and the rest (`realParts`).
- A start's water is a pool (#1218): a place is built as before, and one whose start then has no pool (`spring.ts` `startPool`: its water within the walk a thin sheet, or none) is built again under the pool rule (`tools/real-places.ts` `runPlace`, `poolRule`): `start.water` then fails for a start without a pool, so every step that looks for the start's water (its moves, its springs, the springs' search's `keep`) looks for a pool, while no step may take a thin sheet from it (`NO_WATER`). Under it, real water first: the bodies of real water nearest the start (30 tiles or more of the observed-water mask, within 64 tiles, three at most) are tried as they are, with their bed cut one level down where the survey's land still stands (never twice) and with a source at their head where none stands in them (0.5 and 1 a second), the start where it is and then at the spots nearest the body within 20 tiles' walk of a shore, its wood and berries grown where they fall short (`realPoolFor`); kept where the pool lies in that body, nothing the start passed fails, the water settles, no film grows, no source lands in another's flow and the real water is held. Then the start's spring (`startSpringFor`), then the start moved to one of the six dry spots nearest it whose land fits, with its wood grown and its spring and berries there (`springSpotFor`). The second build is kept where its start has a pool, it fails nothing, gains no fault and holds the water over real water; a place whose start has a pool keeps its build byte for byte. A place kept on today's build (`TODAY_KEPT`) gets the same mends on the committed place, which it then writes, so the next run keeps it. The index's `faults` read the pool rule (a start without a pool has `start.water`, the card's "No reachable water"), as do the places tests. A bed's land given back after the springs' search comes back on the place as it stands (`tools/lib/placeBeds.ts`).
- D302 is the rule for places (planning's ruling of 2026-11-18): an offered start's water is fed by a running source or lasts a Normal drought (`droughtStorageByDay`, 9 days), laid source or real river alike. A holding hollow is preferred for a laid pool when it is within 40 tiles and the start checks hold: a start whose pool a source the tool laid feeds (none of the survey's) and that dries in a Normal drought takes the nearest natural closed hollow within 40 tiles of its middle that, filled to its spill, keeps 0.3 by a shore at the drought's end (four at most), given a spring at its deepest tile (0.5, then 1), the start where it is and then at the twelve spots nearest the hollow; kept where the start has a pool and the one it has at the drought's end lies in the hollow, nothing the start passed fails, none of its numbers falls (room, ways out, moist ground, wood, fast share, berries; its berries planted where a move costs them, never wood), badwater comes no nearer, the water settles, no film grows, no source lands in another's flow and the real water is held (`droughtHollowFor`); far hollows are not built, and a place without one keeps its running source (D302's first arm).
- The survey's ruin squares are taken off before the start, springs and plants are placed (D(new-films)), so a moved or planted tree may stand where a square stood; the ruins are laid again last (`layRuins`).
- Every place carries the data providers' required notices, verbatim (`attribution.ts`).
- The gallery shows every place the tool ships (D445). A place whose start fails the floors (its plants on dry soil cannot move without costing the start its fast wood: no moist ground within its walk) ships no data, card or index entry, only its survey source stays; every `npm run places` prints each such place by name with "start fails the floors" and its numbers, and the summary counts them withheld (since the start's move ranks spots by the land floors and grows their wood, none fails them). A place named in the tool's `WITHHELD_UNTIL` is withheld the same way, printed with its reason, until a fault of its land is mended: Dinaric karst Plitvice, whose start meets the floors but whose conversion puts a source in another's flow (`water.source_in_flow`; a known-fault list never grows to take it back); Twelve Apostles, whose start has no pumpable water within 20 tiles and whose build has faults no known list takes (`water.settles`, a source in another's flow). A place whose start has no water within its walk once every mend is tried, both builds (#1218), is withheld without a name in the list (D(new-films): a start with no water in reach cannot be played), printed with what was tried: Glencoe (its lake, cut and fed, stands above its bank, so no shore a pump reaches) and Ngorongoro (its only water the floor-0 sheet 126 tiles from the start). So "81 offered, 4 withheld: Twelve Apostles, Glencoe, Dinaric karst Plitvice, Ngorongoro". The places tests check every offered place's start by `spring.ts` `startPool` (`PLACES_START_WITHOUT_POOL` in `tests/contract/placesCommon.ts`, a list that only shrinks: Lofoten and Taklimakan Kunlun fan, starts on a thin sheet the first logs can dam, which no mend of #1218 gives a pool). The nightly's `--check` flags their old files if they linger. The index records each place's `faults`; a place with `start.water` carries the card note "No reachable water", and one with `water.settles` (its water keeps moving under the game's rules, D245) "Water keeps moving" (`placeNote`).
- The land has no edge wall (D151): the survey's sealed border is not used. The land is its unsealed crop, the edge runs on past the map as the generator's does (`relaxEdges`), and a short lip stands beside a source the survey put by the edge (`tools/real-places.ts`).
- A start the opened rivers flood, or that reaches no water a source feeds, moves to the best spot its land offers (D245, `start.ts` `placeStartSpots`): dry, water near, the spots whose land gives the start's land floors first (D515: its walkable land, and moist ground for the first farms and a forester within their walk, which no object makes; a lake's shore plate far from the survey's start ranks above a near one too small), then wood and berries kept where it can, never below the starting-logs floor it met. A spot whose water and land meet the floors but whose wood falls below what the start had gets fast trees by it (`tools/real-places.ts` `growFastWood`, pines or birches on the moist ground nearest it) up to `start.wood`'s logs, the starting-logs floor and the fast share: objects only. A start that had water on the survey's sealed land and has none on the open land gets a spring by it (D245's water floor, `spring.ts` `placeSprings`). A start that still reaches no water tries every spot its land offers (a few tiles apart, a shore within 40 tiles' walk) and takes the best that passes `start.water` with the room floor (`start.level_land`) and fails nothing more; where none does, `start.water` stands as the truth of its land, with D445's note. A start short of berries gets the bushes the `start.food` check's own fix plants, on moist tiles within its walk (objects only, D331). A start still short of `start.wood` or the starting-logs floor gets groves of fast trees on the moist ground nearest it (D245's floor met by planting groves, objects only), kept where nothing it passed then fails.
- A place's plants live where they stand (#523): `tools/real-places.ts` moves each living tree or bush on soil that kills it (`plants.survive`'s rule, `doc/plantSoil.ts`) to the nearest free tile within 4 where it lives, or drops it (`tools/lib/placePlants.ts`); the start's berries never fall and the dying trees nearest it stay where they stood, as dead trees (`deadPines`, `deadBirches`, `deadOaks`: the game gives a dead tree its species' logs, which the wood checks count, and `plants.survive` does not count a dead tree), and where its logs are still short they come back as pines (birches where the place leans to them, the fast species `start.wood_fast` counts, D515) on the moist ground nearest it. Objects only: the land and water stay.
- Ruin fields lie along the land (D(new-ruins), `ruins.ts` `placeRuins`): the survey stood its columns in ruled 5×5 squares; a place's fields are laid by the generator's own laying (`resources/baseline.ts` `planRuinFields` with `follow`, D518), each a ragged band 2 to 4 tiles wide along its level's rim with gaps, on the place's levels with their tile-scale speckle taken out (`fieldLevels`: the level most tiles within 2 stand at). The last step of a place's build, on its settled water and soil, which the ruins never change: the place keeps about the columns it had (30,148 of the survey's 30,817), on dry ground first and moist where the dry has no room, never on or beside water, on the start, its door or any object, 22 tiles from the start's middle, each column one a beaver can scavenge (`ruins.access`), seeded from the place's key. A laying that fails a check the place passed is not kept (the next seed is tried). `npm run places -- --ruins` lays only the ruins again, on the committed places (minutes, where the springs' search takes hours), and writes what a full run writes. No ruin field is ruled square (`ruledRuinFields`, tests/contract/places.test.ts).
- Sources only at heads (D171): `tools/lib/placeSources.ts` merges a source that stands in another's flow into the upstream source that feeds it, objects only, when a head stands within 20 tiles (the target's running strength within the game's cap) and the settled depth below it stays within 0.25 (planning's ruling); a row of sources across a river's mouth is one head to the check (`analysis/sources.ts`), so it needs no mend; the rest stay flagged (`PLACES_SOURCES_IN_FLOW` in `tests/contract/placesCommon.ts`).
- The nightly runs `npm run places -- --check`: the data in `public/real-places/` must match a fresh build, or the nightly fails (run `npm run places` and commit). Every run also fails a place whose opened water differs from its file's water settled again (wet tiles within 1 %, no body of 30 or more lost) or that keeps a film above floor 0, with no known list, and prints each place's floor-0 sheet over real water and over real dry land. The build searches start spots and springs, each a full build and settle: 54 to 90 min on one runner since the springs are searched on every place (#1149), so the nightly checks it in five shards, one runner each (`npm run places -- --check --shard i/5`, `tools/lib/placeShards.ts`, dealt by size, with `SHARD_MOVES` moving a place by hand where one shard runs long: Lake Toba from shard 3 to 2): each shard builds its places and compares their data, cards and index entries, and checks the whole set's index and files from what is committed, so the five together say what one full `--check` says. `--times` adds a line under each place of where its time went.
- The rest of the Real places rebuild (round 2: mine sites, badwater) is parked until the map-changing items have settled (D319).

**Start from**: `place.ts` `decodePlaceFile`, `buildPlace` (a place's data to a `.timber`, through `buildFileFromHeights`, which also builds the probe's test maps from heights and objects as the generator's build does); `start.ts` (where a start may move); `spring.ts` (a spring by the start); `attribution.ts` (the credits); the tool's mends in `tools/lib/placeBasins.ts`, `placeSources.ts`, `placePlants.ts` and the water measure in `tools/lib/placeWater.ts` (`npx tsx tools/places-water.ts`).

**Tests**: `tests/contract/places.test.ts`, `places-build-1.test.ts` to `places-build-3.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/places.spec.ts`. The data is written by `tools/real-places.ts` (`npm run places`). Run `npx vitest run tests/contract/places.test.ts`.
