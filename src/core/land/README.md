# land

The generator's land processes (`docs/archive/m9-design.md`): a genome drawn from the theme's prior, then uplift, caprock, erosion and weathering on a height field, snapped to the game's levels, with rivers, lakes, falls and badwater found in its drainage.

**Rules**
- Exact arithmetic only (`num.ts`, PLAN §2.1, D15): + − × ÷, `math/portable.ts` `sqrt`, floor, round, abs, min, max. Angles come from `math/detmath.ts`. Heaps break ties by tile index.
- No river is drawn: rivers follow the eroded field's drainage.
- Intentions are steered, never stamped. A check on the finished map confirms one, and a failed one is dropped, never forced (D138).
- Any change here moves every generated map: re-pin (D308).

**Start from**
- `genome.ts` (the parameter space; `LEANINGS`, `DEFAULT_VARIETY`); `field.ts` `uplift`, `caprock`, `erodeHard`, `weather`; `levels.ts` `snapLevels`, `contourField` (D518: the field softened a tile and bent by a warp before the levels are snapped, so their edges follow warped contours; D544: the graded levels, before rounding, read through a fine two-octave warp, `EDGE_WARP`, 6 tiles of reach over a 6-tile cell at 128² (scaled down with the narrower side below it), so no level's edge runs ruler-straight over a dozen tiles; above 128² the land is drawn in rings (uplands round 4, docs/design/uplands.md; not on Lake Basin and Islands, whose land stays 0.10.48's, `genome.ts` `UPLANDS_BASE_PATH`, round 5): the levels' field is smoothed at the map's scale before its faces are gathered, the grade rolls at the map's scale, the warp is a slow one, `RINGS` (4 tiles over an 18-tile cell, one octave), and `rings.ts` raises ringed hills, mesas and stepped tops 30–80 tiles across on the smoothed levels, and on Highlands and Canyon `bold.ts` (round 5, the bold family) bunches the rings into walls four to six levels tall round named forms (Highlands' mesas with their graded ways up, kept from `notchWalls`; Canyon's gorge side as two faces with a bench between, and hollows off it); the field the water is planned on is not warped; `easeWarpCliffs` then eases every drop of two levels the warp's crowding made where the unwarped levels drop one, so the warp moves the land's own cliffs and makes none); `wander.ts` `wanderEdges` (D518, the look round: every run of one-level steps over 8 sides along one row or column is cut by jogging stretches of it a tile to one side, on the snapped levels, on the land as the washes find it and on the beds' banks; the land keeps its size, no step grows to two levels, nothing moves in the kept-off ground, near water or in the map's last 2 tiles).
- No round form is a circle (the look round of 2026-11-14, item (a): Lake Basin seed 2's south-east mesa stood in step rings with square corners; generator 0.10.24). A disc of a few tiles' radius rasterizes with four flat sides along the rows and columns, and the one-level rings the grade and the erosion draw round its wall repeat that outline. So every round part (`field.ts` `addPart`: a mesa, a plateau, a mesa field's buttes, a cone, each knoll) reads its distance through `formDistance`: the point through a slow warp of a quarter of the radius, the radius lobed by two waves drawn once per form (`outlineOf`: two or three bays and five to seven smaller), swinging about the radius, as far out at the points as in at the bays (generator 0.10.36: waves that only cut in left every form a sixth narrower on average and near a third smaller, and at a high Verticality the mesas and buttes stood in fewer cliffs: Any 96² seeds 1–8 moved 0.164 → 0.188 from 10 to 90 on 0.10.29, against at least 0.03; 0.141 → 0.200 now), and roughened by noise down to two tiles (`FORM_ROUGH` at the most; a form reaches `FORM_REACH` radii at the most), so the edge and every ring parallel to it swing in bays and points with rounded, uneven corners. The weathering's least ground within reach (`weather`, `minFilter`) is read over a disc, not a square: the square's corners cut a second, square ring on every butte's shoulder where the weathering's reach ran out. The disc holds the square's area, (2r + 1)² tiles (generator 0.10.29): a disc of the square's radius left the weathering three quarters of its ground, and the stacks and buttes it wears out of the caprock at a high Verticality wore less, so Verticality's cliffs barely moved from 10 to 90 (0.003 of the map, at least 0.03; `tests/contract/settings.test.ts`). Where Buildable land is above the theme's own (`leanGenome`, `Genome.formsInward`), the waves only cut in and every round form stays inside its radius (generator 0.10.37): at their meant size, a Generous map's level benches stood ringed in the forms' cliffs its walk could not climb, and River Valley 96² seeds 1–8 walked to 3,386 tiles at Generous against Tight's 2,930, at least 500 apart; 4,223 now.
- The land's grade (the showcase round's ground with grade, 2026-11-10): `snapLevels` no longer cuts the field into tabletops a terrace apart. Each genome draws a `Grade` (`genome.ts` `GRADE`, per theme, jittered per map): `mesa`, how much of the map is tableland (a broad noise field, `table`), where the benches keep sharp risers and the rolling is gentle; `riser`, the share of each terrace step that is a graded slope rather than a cliff elsewhere (`levels.ts` `bench`; riser 0 is the old hard step); `drift`, how far the bench levels wander, so crests vary; and `roll`/`rollCell`, a swell noise in levels added before rounding, so the land between rises and falls a level at a time; and `rise`, the rises (D518: sparse compact hills of two or three nested one-level rings, `RISES`). The grade reads `math/noise.ts` `warpedFbm`, never the lattice noise, so no edge is a rectangle; the rolling's two octaves weigh as the lattice's first two did, so the flats keep their size. Terracing raises mesa and lowers riser; a large start area rolls less; Buildable land Generous keeps its benches level with cliffs its ramps climb (no riser, no drift) and all but stops the rolling, Tight is more tableland, and a Relief below the theme's flattens the rolling with it (gone 25 under), and a start water rule under 8 tiles halves it at 4, so the start's shore holds from one attempt to the next (D471's starved start), so each setting still moves its measure (`tests/contract/settings.test.ts`); Verticality away from the theme's own leans the grade too (sheerer risers and more tableland as it rises, graded flanks as it falls), and Waterfalls above the theme's own (`falls` 2) gathers a reach's drops of two levels or more into falls and makes a drop of four or more a cascade of falls a few tiles apart (`hydro.ts`, the knickpoints), a fall at a river's head staying one; at a theme's own settings nothing changes (generator 0.10.22). Highlands keeps the most tableland (its plateaus and cliffs promise), Delta the least.
- `hydro.ts` `planHydro` (rivers, lakes, falls; River Valley's default trunk, when no way in from an upstream edge crosses half the map, is looked for again entering by any edge but the downstream ones, `across`); a pool scoured a level under the bed below a drop is cut only inside banks that hold it, every tile beside it but its outflow at its water or higher and, on an inflow, below the level its own edge lets water out at, else the bed stays, never a raised lip); on a Delta a course with an edge of its own that a fan's arm crosses joins the main river there, its course below becoming one more of the fan's mouths; and at the end of the plan no channel is left lower than its own reach downstream, the reach below cut down to it on to its exit (a planned lake or a held pool starts it afresh) (generator 0.10.27); `courses.ts` `besideCourses`, the level no tile beside a planned channel is lowered below by the later edge relax and basin outlets (`levels.ts` `relaxEdges`, `carveOutlets`), so neither gives a course's water a lower way out, and `inflowEdges`, the level an inflow's own edge is not lowered below by them (a level over the water standing on its course as planned), so a sea it ends in never leaves beside its mouth (D533); round a sea the shelves that rise leave a course's own channel at its level (`gen/generate.ts` `raiseSeaShelves`), so no course is dammed where it crosses one; `hazards.ts` `planBadwater` (the badwater pits; each ditch but Islands' takes a gully's shape, D536: walked tile by tile down its fall line, the field's own way down or the least cut along the lowest ground, toward that line smoothed and swung by a wander, never over 4 tiles along a row or column, in an even 45° stair of 6 or 9 straight at any angle, at least 8 tiles long; its bed widens to 2 or 3 tiles where a noise along it says (`outletSide`, which `features/route.ts` `channelTiles` carves with banks round it, the containment check still reading the route's bed alone as the levee), and its banks step down to it a level a tile, never a one-tile slot; a pit two tiles clear of another's gully; and a gully is a tributary, planning's rulings of 2026-11-15: its sites are found from the water outward, the places whose own fall line, the field's drainage, reaches water D476 and D498 let it join, a gully drawn before, or the map's edge where it may end there, before any other water and within `DITCH_CAP` tiles (40) (a rim whose fewest steps to an end, `ditchSteps`, are past the cap is ruled out without its route search, `ditchReaches` saying whether it reaches water at all, the same sites and routes as searching); they are tried in the order of their ends, a river or lake below the start's reach first, an earlier gully's bed next, the edge only where a pit has no other (planning's ruling: badwater that leaves the map at once is no hazard); a later gully may end in an earlier one's bed, which goes on a tile wider below the join (`outletTo` "ditch", whose banks raise no ground another piece carved, `features/route.ts` `carveChannel`); none stays within 6 tiles of a river, a stream or another gully for 20 tiles or more but its last 6 (`abreastRun`); its bed stays a level over the planned surface of the water it joins until its last 6 tiles, else the site is no site; the generator's replan after a pit another's water reaches keeps off that pit alone, `gen/generate.ts`; a land with no site a fall-line gully serves takes the ditch as before, `BadwaterAsk.ditch`, and the attempt goes on, D536 (14)); `drainage.ts` `drainage`, the one priority flood (the land's, and Carve's downhill guide), and `edgeSpill` (spill levels on whole levels, side to side, as the game's water moves).
- Lake Basin's catchment (`lakeBasin.ts` `leanCatchment`) keeps its rivers modest, and still reads the water settings (D527): River flow Lush lifts its flow's cap by the genome's own Lush-to-Strong step, and River style Braided keeps its braids, the arm drawn on the longest of its rivers' reaches clear of the lakes (`hydro.ts`, a split on another river than the main where the main has none). Waterfalls Off changes a map that has falls; one whose gentle rivers make none at Few already meets it, as Islands' Rivers 0 does where no river comes in from its sea-ringed edges (generator 0.10.25).
- Ruled water (planning's rulings of 2026-11-15; measured by `tools/ruled-water.ts`): `hydro.ts`'s trace joins a course that would run beside one already traced (within `BESIDE.near`, 12 tiles, for `BESIDE.run`, 16 cells) to it where that stretch begins, by a straight link that never climbs, so the streams on one plain gather instead of running down it side by side like drawn lines (a course beside one from its head is not made; beside means on one plain, the nearest course and the ground between it and the cell at most a level over the cell, so courses a ridge parts keep their own valleys; never on a sea's map; a join the course's own rules refuse leaves it as it ran, and a joined course and the tile it joins keep 4 tiles off the edge; `HydroOptions.joins: false` plans without the joins, which `generate.ts` does when a joined plan is refused on a check that names or touches a joined course, `Hydro.joinedRivers`); Delta's fan arms (`deltaCourse`) step to all eight neighbours on the grid (`math/grid.ts` `N8`; `num.ts` `DIRS8` are unit vectors, and their diagonals were never taken, so the arms went by rows and columns in long straight legs).
- A late rescue round's gentle land (D471, from the eighth round, `easedSettings`) is drawn at a small map's own scale (`DrawOptions.ownScale`, generator 0.10.37): under 128² a map otherwise draws a 128²'s count of parts and Relief's spread of levels, which does not shrink with the map, so a 48² map's gentle land still stood 10–12 levels on 48 tiles, and a starved start's place by its water walked to 30–300 tiles where the rescue asks 468. There the parts come in proportion to the area and the levels spread in proportion to the narrower side (a 48² map's to three eighths): a gentle round on Highlands 48² Hard's starved start made a map 15 times in 20, against 1. The first rounds' land is unchanged.
- Above 128² (D504) the water spreads over the whole map, as the soil's reach of it is the same in tiles at every size: `hydro.ts` reads the land by how far the game's moisture spreads from the planned courses (`moistCost`: a tile a step, six a level up), aims to bring half the map within 10 of it, and gives springs toward the land it leaves farthest two more heads for each 128² beyond the first (`REACH_HEADS`), with one river of its own for each 128² (not on River Valley) where that land drains away from the main water; Lake Basin and Generous buildable land take them too. At 128² and under nothing changes.
- The land above 128² (D547, generator 0.10.49, docs/design/uplands.md; a map whose narrower side is over 128, so not a 512×128 or a 128×256; Lake Basin and Islands excepted, `genome.ts` `UPLANDS_BASE_PATH`): the genome's landforms stand at the map's scale (`genome.ts` `atMapScale`: of the ridges, mesas, plateaus, escarpments, cones, calderas, mesa fields and spirals drawn, one in `formScale`² kept and grown by `formScale`, the narrower side over 128); Canyon and River Valley take a plan of the land at its own scale (`plan.ts`: a slow warped field cut into a floor along the trunk and the tiers above it, laid on the uplift by `field.ts` `layPlan`; a gorge's trunk drains, `hydro.ts`, its bed grading to where it leaves the map); the levels are drawn in rings (`levels.ts` `RINGS`, `rings.ts`, `bold.ts`, the bullet above), every long wall notched by graded ways through (`notchWalls`); and the start's slopes follow the ringed land's rules (`features/slopes.ts` `RING_SLOPES`, the field's `ringSlopes`, kept in the project). Highlands' promise asks a high share of 0.35 there, the game's own highlands' (`gen/outcomes.ts`).
- A gorge winds at its map's scale (D542, generator 0.10.43): a course cut `GORGE_CUT` levels or more under its banks whose floor reaches under `GORGE_FLOOR` tiles beside its channel has its walls at its water, and under 128² its meander's wavelength and end taper are drawn at the narrower side's ratio to 128, never under half (`hydro.ts` `gorgeWander`; a river's own wander, `wanderOf`, is a 128²'s, 16 to 40 tiles, and on a 48² map a gorge six levels deep ran 22 tiles in a straight 45° trench down the D8 drainage's line). A shallow course, or one with a broad floor, keeps its wander, and 128² and up keep theirs. A course but the main river is a gorge as well where its bed runs more than `GORGE_CUT` levels under the bank one to two tiles beyond its floor for half its gorge's wavelength or more (`hydro.ts` `deepStretch`, D543, generator 0.10.44): a tributary's planned cut seldom reaches three, its trench's depth coming from the land it crosses. On a Canyon land whose planned promise is missed with such a tributary wound, its next plans leave the tributaries on their own wander (`Genome.hydro.tributariesKept`, set by `gen/generate.ts`'s land screen): such a tributary is often the canyon itself, in a slot a few tiles wide, and wound, its bends and floor open the slot's thinner wall. An arch over a gorge (`forms3d.ts` `gorgeArches`) is looked for across the heading its gorge runs at each crossing, x, y or a diagonal (`courseAxis`, and on a diagonal the crossing along the other axis too), and reads both ways along that heading (`archWay`).
- `minePads.ts` `minePads` (the mine sites' level ground, made as the land is shaped, D363) and `roomMap` (where a start's walk has it on the settled water).
- `shape.ts` `mapShape` (D357: a map 1.5 times as long as wide or more is a long map: its flow, its water's way to the far end, Islands' chain and mainland, Lake Basin's lake and Delta's fan run its length; Any keeps the bowls and seas it draws, as on a square map) and `startAlong` (where each theme's start leans along it: an end or the middle). On a long map (D488) a Delta's length slope has its contours bent along it (`field.ts` `uplift`), Lake Basin draws one of its own layouts (`lakeBasin.ts` `longLakeLayout`: a lake at the downstream end fed down the length, two or three lakes stepping down it, a pair, or a finger lake anywhere along it), and Canyon and Highlands are as small as a square map of their area (`highlands.ts` `smallness`; Canyon under 64 wide keeps its narrow side's). A long map's open sea is drawn inside it (`genome.ts` `inBasin`, `LONG_SEA`), its length, width and place drawn per map, so its own coast wanders from the lip to well inside; Islands' (`LONG_ISLANDS_SEA`) lies off its mainland toward the far end, which it often reaches, so the mainland's end keeps a start clear of the sea's badwater, and its islands are sized by the map's area, three to five chain islands rather than rows of pebbles (the shapes audit of 2026-10-24).
- `washes.ts` `planWashes` (D517): the dry washes and the old bed, one pass for every path: cut where rain would run, ending at water behind a lip, in a dry hollow or behind a lake's lip, off the water's rim, the start's ground (`startKeepOff`) and the caller's keep-off, with fords down and up its banks at its head, every 15 to 25 tiles and wherever it would part the level walk (`cutsWalk`, `rampsInto`), the bed left uncut where no ford rejoins a part; a bed that still parts the walk, or leaves a place a start may take short of its bench, is undone. `bedMask` gives the dead stands their beds. With `canyon` (D518) it cuts Canyon's side canyons: two or three deeper washes, 3 levels down and 4 wide, deepening from the head, with a ford for each stretch they would strand and none at intervals. Every bed follows its line swung from side to side every 14 tiles (`swingAt`, so the swing always drifts past a tread and a line straight across a flat wanders), walked as `treadsOf`'s tread path (each reach 3 tiles at least before it steps aside), its straight reaches cut by `wanderRuns` (a stretch of 2 to 5 tiles a tile off the line wherever a reach runs 7 tiles or more) with a brush 3 wide (a branch 2) that cuts across a straight reach only and is square at its turns (`brushAcross`: a square brush on a straight reach reached past the jogs' ends and the closed teeth filled the rest, a bed ruler-straight for 59 tiles), its teeth closed, and no bed leaves a broad flat (`WASHES.flat`, 600 tiles) in parts all smaller (D518).
- `benches.ts` `widenBenches`: the bench-width floor (no level band under 5 tiles across its fall line: a ridgelet falls to the higher side, a run of thin treads becomes broad benches and one cliff); a premise's relief ends with it (premise/raster/relief.ts), and the grade (#956) may. `bendLevels` (D518) then bends its row and column steps by a slow noise, so a premise's plates hold no square corner (at every size since D540; the grade's turned noise and rises reach a premise's relief over 128² too).
- `intentions.ts` (the set, the nudges, the checks).
- `num.ts`: the small exact helpers (`dist`, `bump`, `unit`, `DIRS8`); distance to a polyline is `math/polyline.ts` `polyDist`. `levels.ts` reuses the shared rules where they agree: `footComponents` is `analysis/regions.ts` `landRegions` with sizes, `cleanPitsAndSpikes` the build's `integrityLevel`.

**Tests**: `tests/unit/genome.test.ts`, plus the `gen/` tests, which build whole maps. Run `npx vitest run tests/unit/genome.test.ts`.

Mine room uses the placement's nearest-footprint distance, from the start's 3×3 to the site's 5×5, rather than a square's corner or a centre-distance allowance. The generator supplies the same keep-off mask as object placement, including lake beds, the border and the square water margin (`validate/playability.ts` `nearWater` at `FLOOD_MARGIN + 1`, one mask for the objects, the pads and the `extras.placement` check).
