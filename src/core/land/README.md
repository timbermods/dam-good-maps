# land

The generator's land processes (`docs/archive/m9-design.md`): a genome drawn from the theme's prior, then uplift, caprock, erosion and weathering on a height field, snapped to the game's levels, with rivers, lakes, falls and badwater found in its drainage.

**Rules**
- Exact arithmetic only (`num.ts`, PLAN §2.1, D15): + − × ÷, `math/portable.ts` `sqrt`, floor, round, abs, min, max. Angles come from `math/detmath.ts`. Heaps break ties by tile index.
- No river is drawn: rivers follow the eroded field's drainage.
- Intentions are steered, never stamped. A check on the finished map confirms one, and a failed one is dropped, never forced (D138).
- Any change here moves every generated map: re-pin (D308).

**Start from**
- `genome.ts` (the parameter space; `LEANINGS`, `DEFAULT_VARIETY`); `field.ts` `uplift`, `caprock`, `erodeHard`, `weather`; `levels.ts` `snapLevels`, `contourField` (D518: the field softened a tile and bent by a warp before the levels are snapped, so their edges follow warped contours); `wander.ts` `wanderEdges` (D518, the look round: every run of one-level steps over 8 sides along one row or column is cut by jogging stretches of it a tile to one side, on the snapped levels, on the land as the washes find it and on the beds' banks; the land keeps its size, no step grows to two levels, nothing moves in the kept-off ground, near water or in the map's last 2 tiles).
- No round form is a circle (the look round of 2026-11-14, item (a): Lake Basin seed 2's south-east mesa stood in step rings with square corners; generator 0.10.24). A disc of a few tiles' radius rasterizes with four flat sides along the rows and columns, and the one-level rings the grade and the erosion draw round its wall repeat that outline. So every round part (`field.ts` `addPart`: a mesa, a plateau, a mesa field's buttes, a cone, each knoll) reads its distance through `formDistance`: the point through a slow warp of a quarter of the radius, the radius lobed by two waves drawn once per form (`outlineOf`: two or three bays and five to seven smaller) and roughened by noise down to two tiles (`FORM_ROUGH` at the most; a form reaches `FORM_REACH` radii at the most), so the edge and every ring parallel to it swing in bays and points with rounded, uneven corners. The weathering's least ground within reach (`weather`, `minFilter`) is read over a disc, not a square: the square's corners cut a second, square ring on every butte's shoulder where the weathering's reach ran out.
- The land's grade (the showcase round's ground with grade, 2026-11-10): `snapLevels` no longer cuts the field into tabletops a terrace apart. Each genome draws a `Grade` (`genome.ts` `GRADE`, per theme, jittered per map): `mesa`, how much of the map is tableland (a broad noise field, `table`), where the benches keep sharp risers and the rolling is gentle; `riser`, the share of each terrace step that is a graded slope rather than a cliff elsewhere (`levels.ts` `bench`; riser 0 is the old hard step); `drift`, how far the bench levels wander, so crests vary; and `roll`/`rollCell`, a swell noise in levels added before rounding, so the land between rises and falls a level at a time; and `rise`, the rises (D518: sparse compact hills of two or three nested one-level rings, `RISES`). The grade reads `math/noise.ts` `warpedFbm`, never the lattice noise, so no edge is a rectangle; the rolling's two octaves weigh as the lattice's first two did, so the flats keep their size. Terracing raises mesa and lowers riser; a large start area rolls less; Buildable land Generous keeps its benches level with cliffs its ramps climb (no riser, no drift) and all but stops the rolling, Tight is more tableland, and a Relief below the theme's flattens the rolling with it (gone 25 under), and a start water rule under 8 tiles halves it at 4, so the start's shore holds from one attempt to the next (D471's starved start), so each setting still moves its measure (`tests/contract/settings.test.ts`); Verticality away from the theme's own leans the grade too (sheerer risers and more tableland as it rises, graded flanks as it falls), and Waterfalls above the theme's own (`falls` 2) gathers a reach's drops of two levels or more into falls and makes a drop of four or more a cascade of falls a few tiles apart (`hydro.ts`, the knickpoints), a fall at a river's head staying one; at a theme's own settings nothing changes (generator 0.10.22). Highlands keeps the most tableland (its plateaus and cliffs promise), Delta the least.
- `hydro.ts` `planHydro` (rivers, lakes, falls; River Valley's default trunk, when no way in from an upstream edge crosses half the map, is looked for again entering by any edge but the downstream ones, `across`); a pool scoured a level under the bed below a drop is cut only inside banks that hold it, every tile beside it but its outflow at its water or higher and, on an inflow, below the level its own edge lets water out at, else the bed stays, never a raised lip); on a Delta a course with an edge of its own that a fan's arm crosses joins the main river there, its course below becoming one more of the fan's mouths; and at the end of the plan no channel is left lower than its own reach downstream, the reach below cut down to it on to its exit (a planned lake or a held pool starts it afresh) (generator 0.10.27); `courses.ts` `besideCourses`, the level no tile beside a planned channel is lowered below by the later edge relax and basin outlets (`levels.ts` `relaxEdges`, `carveOutlets`), so neither gives a course's water a lower way out; `hazards.ts` `planBadwater`; `drainage.ts` `drainage`, the one priority flood (the land's, and Carve's downhill guide), and `edgeSpill` (spill levels on whole levels, side to side, as the game's water moves).
- Lake Basin's catchment (`lakeBasin.ts` `leanCatchment`) keeps its rivers modest, and still reads the water settings (D527): River flow Lush lifts its flow's cap by the genome's own Lush-to-Strong step, and River style Braided keeps its braids, the arm drawn on the longest of its rivers' reaches clear of the lakes (`hydro.ts`, a split on another river than the main where the main has none). Waterfalls Off changes a map that has falls; one whose gentle rivers make none at Few already meets it, as Islands' Rivers 0 does where no river comes in from its sea-ringed edges (generator 0.10.25).
- Ruled water (planning's rulings of 2026-11-15; measured by `tools/ruled-water.ts`): `hydro.ts`'s trace joins a course that would run beside one already traced (within `BESIDE.near`, 12 tiles, for `BESIDE.run`, 16 cells) to it where that stretch begins, by a straight link that never climbs, so the streams on one plain gather instead of running down it side by side like drawn lines (a course beside one from its head is not made); Delta's fan arms (`deltaCourse`) step to all eight neighbours on the grid (`math/grid.ts` `N8`; `num.ts` `DIRS8` are unit vectors, and their diagonals were never taken, so the arms went by rows and columns in long straight legs).
- Above 128² (D504) the water spreads over the whole map, as the soil's reach of it is the same in tiles at every size: `hydro.ts` reads the land by how far the game's moisture spreads from the planned courses (`moistCost`: a tile a step, six a level up), aims to bring half the map within 10 of it, and gives springs toward the land it leaves farthest two more heads for each 128² beyond the first (`REACH_HEADS`), with one river of its own for each 128² (not on River Valley) where that land drains away from the main water; Lake Basin and Generous buildable land take them too. At 128² and under nothing changes. Since D(water-tree) that land is reached by a tree, not a web: `TREE` caps the heads at `3 + 1.5·√(area / 128²)` (6 at 256²) with no reach target, seats each spring where water gathers (a tile draining 0.15–0.6 % of the map), splits the springs' water by the land each course drains, and widens a course with the root of its water and 3 tiles at least below each join. Round 2: a course keeps 3 tiles of land between it and the map's edge but where it comes in or leaves (`TREE.bank`); each head is the one of the first six that trace whose course serves the most dry bench land no course serves yet (`TREE.pick`, `TREE.reach`); a channel wider than `MAX_WIDTH` has rough banks (`TREE.rough`). Round 3: brooks, 2 wide, head on the land farthest from the moisture's reach and join a larger course, 5 for each √(area / 128²) (`TREE.brook`); the trunk is the first course that traces; no rivers of their own on the tree; Islands are off the tree and the beside-join; Delta's courses are 10 wide at most (`TREE.deltaCap`). Round 4: a brook's spring fills its bed and comes out of its basin's water (the first course down from its join that keeps `TREE.run` after it), never on top of it, and no course carries less than `TREE.run`, the springs' least taken off the inflows; pools where the water makes them, below a step and then 5 to 7 channel widths apart along a level reach (`TREE.pool`), at a join, where the bed is widest or on the outside of a bend, else by a seeded draw in that stretch, a width and a half long (`TREE.poolLen`); a basin of `TREE.basin` of the map or more with no course gets a spring of its own beyond the heads' count; and the start weighs the dry land near it that stays moist through the rule's drought on its whole score (`settler.ts` `foodFirst`), so its berries stand on water that lasts. Round 5: the tree re-orders dev's water and does not redistribute it: dev's heads, tributaries and flows, each course wider below a join by the water it brings (`TREE.grow` where one half as wide or more joins); the tree's own heads and water (the cap on heads, seats, the bench pick, the split by drained area, `TREE.run`, brooks, the basin rule and the start chosen for food) are `TREE_PARTS.springs`, off, and its pools `TREE_PARTS.pools`, on.
- `minePads.ts` `minePads` (the mine sites' level ground, made as the land is shaped, D363) and `roomMap` (where a start's walk has it on the settled water).
- `shape.ts` `mapShape` (D357: a map 1.5 times as long as wide or more is a long map: its flow, its water's way to the far end, Islands' chain and mainland, Lake Basin's lake and Delta's fan run its length; Any keeps the bowls and seas it draws, as on a square map) and `startAlong` (where each theme's start leans along it: an end or the middle). On a long map (D488) a Delta's length slope has its contours bent along it (`field.ts` `uplift`), Lake Basin draws one of its own layouts (`lakeBasin.ts` `longLakeLayout`: a lake at the downstream end fed down the length, two or three lakes stepping down it, a pair, or a finger lake anywhere along it), and Canyon and Highlands are as small as a square map of their area (`highlands.ts` `smallness`; Canyon under 64 wide keeps its narrow side's). A long map's open sea is drawn inside it (`genome.ts` `inBasin`, `LONG_SEA`), its length, width and place drawn per map, so its own coast wanders from the lip to well inside; Islands' (`LONG_ISLANDS_SEA`) lies off its mainland toward the far end, which it often reaches, so the mainland's end keeps a start clear of the sea's badwater, and its islands are sized by the map's area, three to five chain islands rather than rows of pebbles (the shapes audit of 2026-10-24).
- `washes.ts` `planWashes` (D517): the dry washes and the old bed, one pass for every path: cut where rain would run, ending at water behind a lip, in a dry hollow or behind a lake's lip, off the water's rim, the start's ground (`startKeepOff`) and the caller's keep-off, with fords down and up its banks at its head, every 15 to 25 tiles and wherever it would part the level walk (`cutsWalk`, `rampsInto`), the bed left uncut where no ford rejoins a part; a bed that still parts the walk, or leaves a place a start may take short of its bench, is undone. `bedMask` gives the dead stands their beds. With `canyon` (D518) it cuts Canyon's side canyons: two or three deeper washes, 3 levels down and 4 wide, deepening from the head, with a ford for each stretch they would strand and none at intervals. Every bed follows `treadsOf`'s tread path (each reach 3 tiles at least before it steps aside), its straight reaches cut by `wanderRuns` (a stretch of 2 to 5 tiles a tile off the line wherever a reach runs 7 tiles or more) with a square brush, 3 wide (a branch 2), its teeth closed, and no bed leaves a broad flat (`WASHES.flat`, 600 tiles) in parts all smaller (D518).
- `benches.ts` `widenBenches`: the bench-width floor (no level band under 5 tiles across its fall line: a ridgelet falls to the higher side, a run of thin treads becomes broad benches and one cliff); a premise's relief ends with it (premise/raster/relief.ts), and the grade (#956) may. `bendLevels` (D518) then bends its row and column steps by a slow noise, so a premise's plates hold no square corner.
- `intentions.ts` (the set, the nudges, the checks).
- `num.ts`: the small exact helpers (`dist`, `bump`, `unit`, `DIRS8`); distance to a polyline is `math/polyline.ts` `polyDist`. `levels.ts` reuses the shared rules where they agree: `footComponents` is `analysis/regions.ts` `landRegions` with sizes, `cleanPitsAndSpikes` the build's `integrityLevel`.

**Tests**: `tests/unit/genome.test.ts`, plus the `gen/` tests, which build whole maps. Run `npx vitest run tests/unit/genome.test.ts`.

Mine room uses the placement's nearest-footprint distance, from the start's 3×3 to the site's 5×5, rather than a square's corner or a centre-distance allowance. The generator supplies the same keep-off mask as object placement, including lake beds, the border and the square water margin (`validate/playability.ts` `nearWater` at `FLOOD_MARGIN + 1`, one mask for the objects, the pads and the `extras.placement` check).
